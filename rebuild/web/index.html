THE PROGRAM, IN PROFILES

Kansas DNA.

Different great Kansas teams won in different ways. These six overlapping roles draw their statistical benchmarks from rotation players on the 2008, 2012, 2018, 2020, and 2022 teams. A player can fit more than one role.

These are descriptive historical profiles, not proven causes of winning. Role definitions are transparent rules; benchmark values come from data. 2018 reflects on-court performance, including subsequently vacated results. 2020 is included despite the canceled tournament.
